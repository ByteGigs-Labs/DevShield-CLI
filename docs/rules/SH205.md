# SH205: Weak or empty application secret

**Severity:** high · **Category:** secrets · **Applies to:** all projects

## Why it matters

Session, JWT and encryption keys that are empty, short or well known let attackers forge sessions and tokens or decrypt data.

## How to fix

Generate a random value of at least 32 characters (Laravel: `php artisan key:generate`).

## Ignoring this rule

Skip it everywhere in `devshield.yml`:

```yaml
ignore:
  - SH205
```

Or skip one line with a comment on that line or the line above:

```text
devshield-ignore: SH205
```
