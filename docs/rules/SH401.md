# SH401: S3 bucket is publicly accessible

**Severity:** high · **Category:** security · **Applies to:** cloudformation, terraform

## Why it matters

A public S3 bucket lets anyone on the internet read, and sometimes write, its objects. Public buckets are a leading cause of data leaks.

## How to fix

Use a private ACL, enable all four settings of the public access block, and grant access with specific principals or CloudFront origin access control.
