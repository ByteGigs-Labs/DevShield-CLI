# SH106: Session cookies not hardened

**Severity:** medium · **Category:** authentication · **Applies to:** aspnet, django, fastapi, flask, spring

## Why it matters

Cookies without the Secure flag travel over plain HTTP, and cookies without HttpOnly can be read by injected scripts, so a single XSS bug or open network leaks sessions.

## How to fix

Enable the Secure and HttpOnly flags for session and CSRF cookies.
