# SH109: NestJS request validation not enabled

**Severity:** medium · **Category:** authentication · **Applies to:** nestjs

## Why it matters

Without a ValidationPipe, the decorators on your DTO classes are ignored and request bodies reach your handlers unchecked, which invites injection and mass-assignment bugs.

## How to fix

Add `app.useGlobalPipes(new ValidationPipe({ whitelist: true }))` in main.ts, or register it with the APP_PIPE provider.
