# SH104: Laravel session cookie not hardened

**Severity:** medium · **Category:** authentication · **Applies to:** laravel

## Why it matters

Session cookies without secure, http_only and same_site can be sent over HTTP, read by scripts, or attached to cross-site requests.

## How to fix

In config/session.php set secure=true, http_only=true and same_site='lax'.
