# Code of Conduct

We want DevShield to be a welcoming project. Everyone taking part is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Keep discussion on the technical matter at hand.
- Not harass, insult or discriminate against anyone.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules and
may ban repeat offenders. Report problems through a private GitHub security advisory
or by contacting the maintainers directly.

This policy is a short form of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
